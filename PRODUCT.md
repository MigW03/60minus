# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using macOS who type number words in everyday text fields and want an immediate, keyboard-friendly conversion to digits without leaving their current application or interrupting their typing flow.

## Product Purpose

60Minus is a native macOS number-word corrector. It recognizes completed number phrases, offers the intended digits beside the caret, and applies a chosen correction in place. Success means the correction feels like a built-in macOS writing assistance feature: immediate, dependable, quiet, and available across accessible applications.

## Brand Personality

Native, restrained, and trustworthy. The product should feel at home beside Apple's spelling and text-correction interfaces, with no visual branding competing with the user's work.

## Anti-references

Avoid web-style cards, oversized typography, decorative color, custom-looking buttons, persistent chrome, and popovers that resemble a third-party overlay. Avoid visual choices that make the menu feel detached from macOS.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the typing flow and never take keyboard focus from the target application.
2. Prefer established macOS controls, materials, metrics, and interaction conventions.
3. Show only the information needed to understand and accept a correction.
4. Keep feedback immediate and placement spatially connected to the caret.
5. Respect system appearance, accessibility settings, and input privacy by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow macOS accessibility conventions, system appearance, increased contrast, reduced transparency, keyboard navigation, and readable system text sizing. Secure and password fields remain excluded. Selection must not rely on color alone.
